Contact Sheet Plan - 10 Slides, 6 Distinct Macro Layouts

1. COVER: Full-bleed hero image with bold title overlay
2. WHY: Split layout - flavor diagram left, benefit text right
3. INGREDIENTS: 2x3 ingredient grid with visual icons/photos
4. PREP: Side-by-side prep comparison layout
5. STEP 1: Left-to-right process timeline with action images
6. STEP 2: Left-to-right process timeline with action images (variant)
7. STEP 3: Central combination diagram with callouts
8. TIPS: 2x2 tip card grid
9. PLATING: Full-bleed hero image with minimal text overlay
10. CLOSING: Centered text with warm background accent

Layout Rhythm:
- Slides 1,9: Full-bleed heroes (bookend pattern)
- Slides 2,8: Split/grid information layouts
- Slides 5,6,7: Process sequence trio (varied compositions)
- Slides 3,4: Ingredient/prep layouts
- Slide 10: Clean closing

No 3 consecutive slides share the same macro layout.
